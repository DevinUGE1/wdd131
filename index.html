Huanta, Perú

Peru is a country located on the western coast of South America, known for its rich history, diverse geography, and vibrant cultural traditions. It is home to the ancient Inca civilization, the famous Machu Picchu, the Andes Mountains, and part of the Amazon rainforest, making it one of the most historically and naturally diverse countries in the world. In the Ayacucho region of Peru is the city of Huanta, often called “The Emerald of the Andes” because of its beautiful green landscapes and pleasant climate. Huanta is known for its agricultural traditions, welcoming community, and cultural celebrations that reflect the rich heritage of the Peruvian Andes.
