Restaurante Do Peruano - Site Plan

Planning Document for WDD131 Project

Site Name

Restaurante Do Peruano

This name reflects a Peruvian restaurant identity in Portuguese, emphasizing authentic cuisine and cultural heritage.

Optional Domain: restaurantedoperuano.com

Site Purpose

The website will promote a fictional Peruvian restaurant and provide visitors with menu information, cultural background, and reservations.

It will include pages such as Home, Menu, About, and Contact with interactive JavaScript features.

Scenarios

  • What are the most popular traditional Peruvian dishes served?
  • How can I make a reservation or find the restaurant’s hours?

Color Schema

  • #C62828 – Headers, navigation, accents
  • #F9A825 – Highlights and buttons
  • #FFF8E1 – Background
  • #2E2E2E – Body text

Typography

  • Roboto – Body text and general UI
  • Georgia – Headings for a traditional feel
